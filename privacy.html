Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Fatha Games ("we", "our", or "us") develops and operates mobile games and applications distributed via platforms including the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected when you interact with our applications.

Information Collection

We do not require account registration, and we do not directly collect or store personal identity data such as your real name, email address, physical address, or precise GPS location.

In-App Purchases

Our games may offer optional in-app purchases and subscriptions. All payment transactions are handled exclusively by the platform provider:

  • Google Play In-App Billing (for Android devices)
  • Apple In-App Purchase (for iOS devices)

We do not collect, process, or store credit card details or financial information. All billing data is governed by the respective privacy policies of Google and Apple.

Third-Party Services & Analytics

To ensure technical stability, analyze gameplay performance, and display advertisements, our games may integrate trusted third-party SDKs, which may automatically process anonymous or pseudonymous device data (such as device model, OS version, IP-derived approximate country, session duration, and crash logs). These providers include:

  • Google AdMob: For serving in-game ads.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase & Firebase Realtime Database: For gameplay analytics, configuration, and crash diagnostics.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Services: For core platform functionalities and achievements.

Advertising & Tracking Choices

Advertisements may utilize standard mobile advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS).

  • On iOS: In accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, tracking across third-party apps and websites for ad targeting is subject to your explicit permission. You can change this at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android: You can reset or opt out of personalized ads under Settings > Google > Ads.

Children’s Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Our games are designed for general audiences and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal data has been inadvertently collected, please reach out to us and we will promptly delete it.

User Rights & Data Deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to request access to or deletion of data associated with your device identifiers. Since we do not maintain user accounts, you may contact us with your device diagnostic ID to request assistance regarding third-party log deletion.

Data Security

We take reasonable industry-standard measures to safeguard all technical data handled by our applications. However, please note that no electronic transmission or cloud storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL, and the "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect the latest version.
